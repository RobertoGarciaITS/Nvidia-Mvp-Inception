# NVIDIA Inception Slide Content Specification v0.1

**Project:** Human Intelligence Platform  
**Audience:** NVIDIA Inception reviewers, technical and business stakeholders  
**Purpose:** Source of truth for slide copy, evidence, claims, visuals, and design authorization  
**Status:** CONTENT SPECIFICATION / RESEARCH REQUIRED  
**Assessment date:** 2026-10-02

## Usage rule

This document defines what the design agent may show. It does not authorize invented numbers, traction, customers, benchmarks, NVIDIA implementations, or market claims. Any `TBD`, `UNVERIFIED`, or `PENDING` field must remain visibly qualified until evidence is added.

Claim labels:

`[F]` Fact · `[E]` Evidence · `[H]` Hypothesis · `[A]` Assumption · `[I]` Inference · `[P]` Projection · `[V]` To be validated
